Austin ride-share pickups

Uber, Lyft, Waymo and RideAustin report every pickup to the city through Mastic. The map shows only what the city learns.

    Click a company to shade cells by its share.

    What the city learns

    Each company turns each ride into a Mastic report: the pickup cell (α) and a one-hot over the four companies (β). The two aggregators find the cells with at least the threshold number of rides without seeing any single report.

    For each heavy cell the city learns one count per company. It learns nothing about individual rides or riders, nothing about cells below the threshold, and nothing beyond these counts. The colours come only from those counts.

    Companies are assigned synthetically (city/make_company_rides.py); the pickups are real RideAustin data from the busiest month (Mar 31 – Apr 13, 2017).
